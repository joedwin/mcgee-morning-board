# McGee Morning Board

A one-page weekday departure board for getting between North Berkeley and Salesforce Tower: the transbay buses under 37 minutes from the closest stop on each AC Transit line (J and FS both from Sacramento & University, G from San Pablo & Cedar), plus the next BART trains. Morning and evening tabs, Bus-only or Bus + BART.

Ten looks, switchable from the picker: Classic, Split-flap, Map (schematic), Sky (follows the real Berkeley sky), Type, Real map (OpenStreetMap with true route geometry), Strips, Radar, River (timeline), Bay (silhouette). The map-style looks draw vehicles live along the routes.

- Static HTML, no build step. Open `index.html` or host it anywhere. Deep links: `?look=strip`, `?mode=bus`, `?dir=pm`.
- Tap any departure for a trip sheet: the route as a stylized stop list with timepoints, your boarding stop and exit stop, and the vehicle's live position (the bus is linked to the trip through the AC Transit prediction's vehicle id; with a token saved, the live pattern fills in every stop). BART trips show the stations, the transfer point, and the train's live approach.
- Laid out for phones (checked on an iPhone 13 mini): safe-area padding, a scrolling look picker, the trip sheet as a bottom sheet with swipe-to-dismiss, and the map looks re-scaled or pannable at 375 px.
- BART real-time comes from the public BART API and works out of the box. Train positions are derived from arrival estimates at the far end of the leg.
- AC Transit real-time (predictions and bus positions) needs a free personal key from https://api.actransit.org/transit/Account/Register. Paste it into the "See this bus live" box in any bus trip's sheet, or under About; it is stored only in that browser. To set up another device, use About → "Copy setup link" (the key rides in the `#token=` fragment, is saved on arrival, and is removed from the address bar). Requests try a normal `fetch` first and fall back to JSONP (every `actrealtime` endpoint takes a `callback`) if the browser blocks the cross-origin call.
- Live positions: the trip sheet polls every 20 s while it is open (45 s otherwise), glides the bus to its new spot, and says how old the position is. Predictions are matched to timetable rows by AC Transit's own scheduled time (`schdtm`), and a bus is tied to your trip by its trip id. Without a key the sheet shows a dashed "timetable position · not live" marker instead.
- Timetables: AC Transit J (Dec 2023), FS (current Realign schedule via the actransit.org stop pages, Sep 2026), G (Feb 2026); BART weekday schedule. Route shapes from the AC Transit GTFS feed. Stops that aren't printed timepoints (FS at University & Sacramento, G at San Pablo & Cedar) show ~ estimated times.
- Stop IDs in the page are AC Transit's public five-digit IDs (the ones on the stop signs), which is what the real-time API keys predictions and pattern points by.
