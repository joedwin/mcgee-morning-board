About these times

Evening trips leave the Salesforce Transit Center (J from Bay 35, G from Bay 12) and BART from Montgomery St. Arrival is the closest stop to home; ~ marks stops that aren't printed timepoints, estimated from the nearest one.

J, FS and G are express lines with no local riders and no weekend service. Distances are straight-line from home.

Schedules: J (Dec 2023), FS (Dec 2021), G (Feb 2026), all posted as current on actransit.org; BART times from the BART schedule for North Berkeley → Montgomery.
