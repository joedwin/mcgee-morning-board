About these times

Evening trips leave the Salesforce Transit Center (J from Bay 35, FS from Bay 10, G from Bay 12) and BART from Montgomery St. Arrival is the closest stop to home (the FS drops you at University & Sacramento eastbound, stop 56667); ~ marks stops that aren't timepoints, where the time is an estimate.

J, FS and G are express lines with no local riders and no weekend or holiday service. Mornings leave out bus trips longer than 37 minutes. Distances are straight-line from home.

Times come from AC Transit's and BART's official timetable feeds, rebuilt every night, so schedule changes and holidays show up on their own. For any day the feeds don't cover, the board falls back to times typed from the printed J, FS and G timetables and a regular BART pattern.
